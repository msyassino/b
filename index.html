Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I get started?

Simply create an account, complete your profile, and start browsing available offers. Each completed offer earns you coins that can be redeemed for rewards.

How do I earn coins?

You can earn coins by completing offers, playing games, taking surveys, watching videos, and referring friends. Visit the Earn page to see all available opportunities.

How long do rewards take to process?

Most rewards are processed instantly. Gift cards and crypto withdrawals may take 1-3 business days. If your reward is delayed, please contact support.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal varies by method. PayPal requires 5,000 coins ($5), while crypto methods require 10,000-25,000 coins depending on the cryptocurrency.

Why was my account flagged?

Accounts may be flagged for suspicious activity such as using VPNs, creating multiple accounts, or completing offers fraudulently. Please review our Anti-Fraud Policy and contact support if you believe this is an error.

Is the platform free to use?

Yes, the platform is completely free. You earn coins by completing offers from our partners. Optional coin purchases are also available for faster rewards.

My coins are not showing up after completing an offer.

Some offers take up to 24-48 hours to credit. If your coins still have not appeared after 48 hours, please submit a support ticket with your offer details.

How do I change my email address?

Go to your Profile page and click Edit Profile. You can update your email address there. A verification link will be sent to your new email.
