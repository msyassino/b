Frequently Asked Questions

What is ReWords?

ReWords is a rewards platform where you earn coins by completing offers, playing games, taking surveys, and watching videos. You can then redeem your coins for real rewards like PayPal cash, gift cards, and game top-ups.

How do I earn coins?

There are multiple ways to earn: Complete offers from offerwalls, play game offers and reach milestones, take surveys, watch rewarded ads, check in daily, refer friends, participate in challenges, and complete tasks.

What is the coin conversion rate?

10,000 Coins = $1.00 USD. This rate is fixed across the platform.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Go to Wallet or Withdraw page, select your preferred method (PayPal, Crypto, or Mobile Top-Up), enter the amount (minimum 5,000 coins), and submit your request.

Why was my offer not credited?

Offers may take up to 24 hours to be credited. If your offer has not been credited after 24 hours, please submit a support ticket with the offer details.

How does the referral system work?

Share your unique referral link. You earn 15% of everything your referrals earn. Plus, you both earn bonus coins when they reach milestones.

What is the daily streak bonus?

Log in and claim your daily reward every day. The longer your streak, the higher your rewards and earnings multiplier.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption and security measures. We never sell your personal data.
